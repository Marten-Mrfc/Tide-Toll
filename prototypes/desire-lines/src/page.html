How to play

Villagers walk from their houses to the well, the field and the market, and wear the grass into trails. You never draw a road: draw fences to push the trails aside, put a gate in a fence to let walkers through (slowly), brush gravel where you want a young trail to run (it never grows back), or lay a footbridge across the river. Unused paths grow back.

With Flow preview on, a tool shows the typical routes that would change: dashed is now, solid is after, faint where they run together.

Rain turns worn trails to mud, which walkers avoid where they can; gravel never turns to mud. New families build cottages beside busy trails, so the town grows where you steer the paths.
