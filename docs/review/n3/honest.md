# What got better and what did not, in my honest view

First draft from the log's "Honest view, round by round" (asset round 1 at 22:44, the layout at 23:53). The lead rewrites
this after the final pictures. `make_closing.py` reads the two lists below: every line that starts with "- " under
"## Better" and under "## Not better". Other lines are ignored.

## Better

- The town is clearly better to look at: people instead of pawns, a street that does not repeat, a real mill, trees of five kinds, fields that read as crops.
- The screen is calm now, judged from the pictures: every kind of thing has one place, and nothing overlaps on any of the eight window and interface sizes tested.
- The shop reads as a list with prices instead of twelve cards.
- Play is unchanged by the numbers: the charter at minute 33.9 and 201 purchases, as before the night.

## Not better

- Loads are a few pixels at the normal view and read by colour more than by shape.
- Every roof is the same thatch yellow.
- The charter stone could not be found at a glance after the first round (round 2 made it taller and set it beside the bell).
- With eight things affordable, the shop is a wall of gold pills.
- The left sheet is mostly blank paper early in the game.
- Icons are still stand-ins (round 3 is making them).
- Zoomed out to the book, the one-line purse lies over the page's title.
- Nobody has played the new interface with their own hands.
