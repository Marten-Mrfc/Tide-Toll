# Interface audit 1: first age, 1920 x 1080, interface 100 % (u01 to u18)

Reviewer 1. Judged from the pictures in `build/n3/ui-audit/1920x1080-100/` and the mechanical report next to them. No code read beyond the inventories. "Things asking for attention" = separate cards, banners, notes, tags and floating numbers on screen at once (map labels and the +coin floaters counted only when they are big enough to compete). Second-age rows in section 2 come from the inventory and report, not from pictures I looked at.

The short answer to "why does it feel chaotic": four edges each carry a different, constantly changing stack (left: ledger + hint + request + notes; right: seven to eleven shop cards; top centre: banner, choice, mode banner; bottom: bell, achievements, pause, Home), every stack grows and shrinks on its own clock, nothing tells the eye which one matters now, and everything is the same cream card.

## 3. The ten worst layout problems, worst first

1. **The left column is a stack with no rank.** Ledger, hint, request or drive card and up to three notes are all cream cards of one width, and every card's top is set by the one above it, so a note or hint arriving moves the whole stack. Pictures: u03, u05, u06, u10, u11, u13, u16. A calm layout would give each job its own fixed slot (money, goal, notes), never pushing each other.
2. **The shop is a column of 7 to 11 near-identical cards that re-flows with every state.** Dead rows ("level 3", "done") and live rows look nearly the same and the headers jump. Pictures: u01, u05, u06, u07, u10, u13, u14. A calm layout would show only what can be bought now or next, in fixed rows, with the one affordable item marked clearly and sold-out/locked collapsed.
3. **Top centre has three claimants (level banner, choice card, mode banner) that overlap each other.** Pictures: u06 (39,591 px² overlap), u07, u08, u04, u16. A calm layout would have one message place and a queue: one thing at a time, the choice as a real dialog.
4. **One goal, four places.** "Next:" in the ledger, the level banner, map labels ("A cottage, at level 2") and the hint say the same thing in different words in different places. Pictures: u01, u04, u05, u16. A calm layout would have one "goal" slot that always holds one sentence.
5. **Modes (Pave, Extend) shout from four places at once** (banner, lit shop card, tip, "Suggested" tag) with two different prices on screen. Pictures: u07, u08. A calm layout would have one banner with the mode, one price tip at the pointer, and the shop card unchanged.
6. **Notes, hints and banners are one look: same cream card, same size.** Nothing says "this one matters". An achievement and a charter look the same. Pictures: u02, u03, u05, u10, u11, u13, u16. A calm layout would have three clear kinds (info, reward, warning) in one place with one length of stay.
7. **Layout jumps in one frame.** Ledger height (151, 199, 224 px), request card slot (221, 246), notes (up to 150 px), bell width (175, 274, 265), shop headers and Pause to the other corner at Page stage. Pictures: u02 to u06, u09, u16, u17. A calm layout would reserve the space for each part and fade things in place.
8. **Achievements page is see-through and sits on the map's middle.** Text is crossed by roofs and walkers, the live HUD is still at full strength around it, the close button is in the middle of a field. Pictures: u15. A calm layout would use an opaque page with a dimmed background and its own close button at a corner.
9. **Four corners and edges used with no rule.** Money top left, shop right, bell bottom left, achievements and pause bottom right, Home bottom centre, next-age above the bell, tide dial top left beside money. The eye runs round the whole frame. Pictures: every town picture, u01 and u16 clearest. A calm layout would have a named zone for each kind (top bar, right rail, bottom bar) and keep each kind there in every state.
10. **Information is repeated and large numbers are everywhere.** "Ring in the next age" three times (banner, ledger, button); three numbers per shop card; floaters bigger than any HUD text; the purse in two styles (26 px bold, then the slim strip). Pictures: u16, u10, u13, u11, u17. A calm layout would say each thing once, in one number style, with floaters small and near their source.

## 4. What already works and must be kept

- The map itself: the town, roads, walkers and plots are readable and the HUD leaves the middle free in most pictures (u01, u02, u04, u10 "middle third free" in the report).
- The ledger's top block (coin + rate, level, bar) is a clear unit: u01 to u16, same place, same shape.
- The shop's four headers and the icon at the left of every card; the first column of icons makes cards scannable (u01).
- The affordable card is lit (thick dark border, white fill) so the player can find the next buy (u02, u03, u07).
- Right edge for buying and left edge for status is a good split; it should stay.
- Book and Page stages (u17, u18) are calm: two pages, one slim strip. The paper look suits the game.
- Plain English text, short lines ("A new villager moves in.").
- The request card's bar and "Reward: n coin" give the goal a clear shape (u05, u11).
- The charter drive card (u14) with only the ledger above it is readable.
- Map labels on stakes ("A field, at level 9") tell the player what the plot is, in the world, not in the HUD.
- Floaters showing coin earned at the place it is earned (u02, u10) are a good idea, just too large.
