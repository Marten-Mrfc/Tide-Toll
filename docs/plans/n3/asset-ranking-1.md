# Asset ranking 1 (buildings, people, fields, animals, trees, reeds, roads, ground)

Reviewer 1. Judged from pictures: `build/n3/assets-audit/before/sheet-*.png`, the single -normal and -close pictures, and `build/n3/ui-audit/1920x1080-100/u10, u13, u18` (u32 and u34 do not exist). Style facts are in `style-facts.md`. OWNER = on the owner's list.

The general verdict first. The models are tidy, correctly proportioned boxes and prisms with a good ink line. They fail the Living Map in four ways: (1) everything is one flat orange or brown from above with no texture, course or edge detail, so a roof is a plain tile of colour; (2) the same few models repeat identically (one oval tree, one cottage, one stall) so nothing looks hand drawn; (3) people and loads from above are barrel shapes and beehives, not little figures; (4) the town has no life of its own: no smoke, no birds, no washing, no animals walking, no water movement. Props that fix some of this already exist in `assets/models` (washing line, hen coop, beehives, handcart, flower bed, bench, signpost) but I see none in the town pictures u10, u13.

Ink: 2 to 3 px at play zoom, on everything the same, so a 1 m prop gets the same line as a 12 m house. At the normal view the ink does more of the drawing than the shape does.

## 2. What is good (the standard to match)

1. **well** (and the bell post): a strong silhouette from above (octagon with blue water and a gabled roof), three clear palette colours, ink only on the shape, size right against the villager (2.4 m against a 2.9 m person). Every other building should have this much shape and this few colours.
2. **bell-post**: a silhouette that cannot be mistaken, a single warm roof, a green bell, stone feet. One idea per model.
3. **keeper-hut / market stall awning / the apple tree**: little, well-detailed, finished on every side (door, lamp, bench, chimney), with colour contrast against Meadow. The red and cream stripe is the one place where the palette sings; reuse that kind of contrast (Brick, Limewash, Cast-iron green) in other roofs and cloth.
(Also good: cobbles with ink joints, the washing line and hen coop props, the open book page.)

## 3. Missing from the town (twelve and more)

Where: on the ridge, east gable. Reads at once as a living house; no chimney model exists (`TownBuildings.cs:162`).
2. **Birds**: 5 to 12 tiny ink-V swallows (0.5 m span) circling the mill and bell, plus a 3 m flock of 8 rooks that lifts off a field when villagers pass. Where: over fields and the green.

## Priority for a builder (the five that change the look most)

1. Villager and loads (they are what the eye follows).
2. Trees (they frame everything).
3. Extended cottage as real geometry plus chimney and chimney smoke.
4. Fields with volume plus hedgerows and gardens.
5. Mill body and ground ink language (trail edges, grass tufts, river shimmer).
