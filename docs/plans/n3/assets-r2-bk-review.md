## 2. Things in place

**The table and the book: more beautiful than before.** Clearly. Before: a flat near-black field under a book. After: warm planks with grain, knots, joints, a ribbon running off the page, a pot with a quill and a seal at the corners. The book frame now looks like a desk. What still spoils it:
- At the normal view, the table's top-left corner shows the ink pot cut off by the screen edge (book-desk-normal, top-left): half a bowl of white and a grey tab, which reads as a stray rectangle. Keep the ink pot fully inside the view or move it 40 m inward.
- The seal at the bottom right of that picture is half out of the frame too, looks like a red dot.
- Close up (book-table-close) the wood turns into stretched smears.
- The closed book is a big flat dark red square with little to read at N. It reads as a "box", not a leather book; the blind tooling and the clasp are faint.
- The map page in book-open-normal is empty (no town yet) so the new page cannot be judged; the old before picture showed a town. This is a state difference, not a fault of the asset, but the picture does not prove that the paper and table look good with a full page.

**Plot stakes in the town (town-plot-stakes-normal/close): better, but too weak.** The black frame is gone, which was the big fault. Now the stakes are a faint pale line on green; at the normal view the plot is easy to miss, but the label "The market, to buy" helps. Close up the pennant and caps read fine. The rope is thinner than the stakes' shadow; at the page it will vanish.

**Charter stone in place (town-charter-stone-normal/close): better.** The 3.4 m stone with a red seal stands on a stone patch in front of the bell frame and is found at a glance in the close picture. At the normal view the stone is slightly small beside the bell frame (about half the frame's height) and a shadow patch (dark smear on the bell house floor) sits behind it. Fine, but raise the stone to 4 m to be the civic mark of the first age. The wooden bench beside it is clipped by the stone's stone patch.

**Town views (town-view-charter, town-view-level-5):** the well, bell green and lane are the same as before; none of the BK props appear in either view (no flower bed, signpost, coop, cart, sheep in any town picture). So the props' improvement cannot be judged in place from this run. Say so to the lead: add them to the town audit or place them.

## 3. MUST FIX (worst first)

1. **Handcart: rebuild.** Unchanged from before and the wheels, sack and handles do not read at N or C. Round standing wheels 0.9 m, sack 0.6 m tied, 0.2 m handles.
2. **Sheep: rebuild.** Pyramid lumps and a red box head; unreadable at N and a toy-block at C. One round fleece, small dark face, thin legs.
3. **Closed book front: not readable at N.** A blank red field. Raise tooling contrast and add a gilt device or title plate.
4. **Plot stakes: too faint at P and N.** Add Brick caps, a thicker rope with ink edge and a red pennant larger than 0.5 m.
5. **Wattle fence and fence post: no gain.** The post is a dark dash, the wattle a crumpled strip. Cap the post with a 0.3 m Paper cap; rebuild the wattle with five clear vertical withies and gaps.
6. **Flower bed too small at N.** 2.2 x 1.2 m, blossoms 0.35 m.
7. **Hen coop hens do not read.** Make hens with heads, combs and tails; enlarge the coop's front.
8. **Desk things cut at the view edge** (ink pot top-left, seal bottom-right at the normal view): move them 40 m inward, and give the candle a flame.

## SHOULD FIX

- Table texture at close range: double the texture size, thin the black joint.
- Corner pieces on the book overhang the board; inset them 0.15 m.
- Keeper's hut: the bell-cote covers the chimney from above; move it to the gable.
- Pasture ground is a dark olive slab; lighten it and give the shelter thatch courses.
- Remove the grey-blue label square on the quill.
- Charter stone slightly bigger (4 m) and clear the bench's overlap.
- Put BK props in a town picture (cart, signpost, bed, coop, sheep) so they can be judged in place.
- RiverLayer stretches the new stakes (builder's note): use FittedStakes there.
