# Interface audit 2: second age, book, pause and settings (1920x1080, 100 %)

Reviewer 2. Judged from the pictures `build/n3/ui-audit/1920x1080-100/` u17 to u25, u30 to u41, with u03, u10, u13 as the first-age baseline. The mechanical report (`report.md`) was read for overlaps, cut-offs and moves. No code changed.

How "things asking for attention" was counted: every separate box, button, banner, note, floating number, map label and map ring on screen at once (a shop card counts one; a list row in a book table does not count, the table counts one). It is a count of what the eye has to sort, not of what matters.

Honest limit first: **u21, u22, u23 and u24 do not show settings at all.** The pictures are the first-age book spread (u21 and u23 are the same picture, u22 and u24 are the same picture). The JSON for them lists 39 to 109 settings items, so the settings pages exist in the tree but were not on screen when the picture was taken. Only u25 shows a settings page (Keys). I judge settings from u25 alone and say so below.

## 3. Ten worst layout problems

1. **One left column that grows and pushes.** Ledger (with hint), then request or drive card, then up to 3 notes, all stacked by hand at x 16; the hint line appearing moves everything below by 20 to 60 px (request y 221 in u30, 173 in u38) and the stack reaches 570 px of cards. Shown in u03, u10, u13, u30, u33, u35, u36, u38. A calm layout would give each of ledger, goal and messages a fixed zone with a fixed height, so nothing below it moves when text changes.
2. **Messages come in at least four shapes and nobody knows which matters.** A plain slip, a gold slip, a banner, a hint line, a floater, a tooltip and a mode banner say things like "Milestone!", a boat's demand, a hint or an instruction in the same places and the same fonts. Shown in u03, u10, u13, u33, u35, u36. A calm layout would have two places only (one banner for what is happening now, one notes list for what happened), each with one look.
3. **Gold means five things.** Milestone slips, boat demands with a clock, "The baker is pleased", the banner, the drive card and the floaters all use the same tan or gold. Shown in u10, u13, u33, u35. Gold should mean exactly one thing (for example "act now") and everything else a plain card.
4. **The shop column is the biggest element and it moves.** 10 cards at 280 px wide down the right side, headings bare on the map, rows lock and unlock so the groups jump (Work at y 123, 144, 166), text shrinks to 20 px rows when full (u35). Shown in u03, u10, u13, u30, u35, u38. A calm layout would give each group a fixed block with a fixed number of rows and give the headings a card behind them.
5. **Bare headings and map labels with no card.** "People", "Work", "Roads", "Town", "The river" are printed on the map and are cut by trees or the river (u10, u30, u33, u35); plot labels ("A cottage, at level 18") are struck by buildings (u30). Every text that is not in a card needs a card, or must stay off the map.
6. **Pages float over the middle of the map as grey ghosts.** The age page and the achievements page are translucent sheets 820 to 1120 px wide in the middle of the map with the whole HUD still lit behind them; text is printed through roads and floaters (u31, u38, u39). A calm layout would dim the whole town and HUD behind one opaque page, the same one the book uses.
7. **The second age adds three new places beside the old ones.** Tide card top left beside the ledger (the most prominent corner, for the smallest card), "The river age [N]" stacked over the bell, a new "The river" shop group at the bottom of a column that already reaches y 860. Shown in u30, u33, u35. A calm layout would give "time" (tide, bell, request timers) one strip and "navigation" (pages, achievements, pause) one corner, in both ages.
8. **Pause and the coin strip jump between stages.** Pause is bottom right in town and bottom left at Page and Book (the first thing the owner looks for is missing on one side); the slim strip appears in a place that was empty. Shown in u03, u17, u18, u40, u41. A calm layout would keep Pause, and the coin and level, in the same corner at every stage.
9. **Page and book edges are cut off.** At the Page stage the compass N and the folio "2" are cut off, the left page's text starts at x 8 against the window edge, the two achievement columns do not line up, and at the Book stage the spread is in three different positions (u18, u21 and u23, u22 and u24). Shown in u17, u21 to u24, u40. A calm layout would put the page inside a margin of at least 40 px and always in the same spot.
10. **Pause, cover and settings are three more styles, and settings was not on screen in four of five pictures.** Cover menu as bare text (barely visible in u19), a leather page with two unequal buttons for one sentence (u20), a grey-beige settings page with four pastel tabs and a dialog that sits on its own "Restore defaults" button (u25). A calm layout would have one paper, one button shape and one dialog, and the settings pages need to be retaken (u21 to u24 show the book).

## 4. What already works and must be kept

- The ledger as one card in the top left corner: coin, income, level, bar, renown, next gift in a clear order (u03, u10, u13). Keep its place and order; fix only its height.
- The shop's three-line card (name and key, price and need, income) with a thin affordability bar under it; "Need 58 more" tells the player what to do. The white card for "affordable now" is a clear signal in u03 and u13.
- The right side for buying and the left side for status: players do learn that quickly. Keep the right edge for the shop.
- The map is mostly free in the centre in u03, u10, u13, u30, u33: the middle third has little over it except floaters and pages. Keep it that way.
- The book spread at the Book stage (u18, u41): framed paper, large title, stats list, achievements table, map on the right page. This is the best looking and calmest picture in the set; the town HUD should look like it.
- The bell card text, the Pave key hints [4 or P] and the "[N]" and "[Esc]" key hints on buttons: they teach the keys where they are used.
- Request cards with a time bar that turns orange (u13, u30): one clear place for one clear task.
- The tide dial with its words "High water falling, lowest in 1:55" (u30): clear in words and in the dial; the problem is only its place and size.
