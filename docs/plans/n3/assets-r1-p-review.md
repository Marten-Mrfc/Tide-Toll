### Cottage a0 (plain, hipped roof) / a1 / a2
- Page: PASS. A square hipped house with a chimney is a house.
- Normal: PASS. Roof, chimney, accent door (blue/green/brick) show.

- Differs: a0 is the only hipped one; the a-family has no gable ends, so its silhouette is a square; PASS as a variant, but see street: the a-roofs are the same yellow trapezoid in every tone.

## 2. Town pictures

Street / wood and field picture (`street-normal`, `street-close`, `town-view-level-8`) and the two town pictures (`town-view-charter`, `town-view-level-8`, `town-cottage-extension-*`):

Better than before? Yes, clearly, for the people and the houses. Before: a blank yellow rectangle and a red pawn; now silhouettes vary (hipped, gabled, L-shaped), each house has a door in an accent colour, a chimney and courses, and villagers have faces, hats, arms and loads. The street picture reads as a street of different houses with people, not as a row of boxes.

What still spoils it, exactly:
1. In the street-normal picture (row of five cottages), the first and last are cropped at the frame edges (the end cottages), so I cannot judge them; the camera for `street` must be pulled back 15 percent.
2. Every roof is the same flat bright Thatch (#C9A55A and its light tone), about 70 percent of every house; the five houses and the eight villagers' hats and grain loads share one yellow, so the picture is a field of yellow rectangles. Needs courses in the shade tone and one house with a Slate roof patch or a Brick-tiled lean-to; also darker ridge bands.
3. Villagers are about 45 px tall beside 90 px houses; they look good, but at the normal view in the town pictures (`town-view-level-8`, `town-view-charter`) the arms and the load are 2 to 6 px and the people are only a coloured dot with a skin spot. The queue at the charter picture (right edge, a dozen villagers in a line) reads as a string of hats and is fine; the loads are not readable there.
4. At the town view the cottage at the road fork (level 8) is three gables stuck together with an orange selection ring and a double chimney; with no smoke visible in any picture (the Smoke markers exist but no puff is drawn) the houses look dead.
5. Fields (not this track): plank pallets with dark seams at the left edge of both town pictures (the biggest shape in the picture and by far the ugliest: striped brown and yellow slab). It drags the whole town down; it is the next thing for track W.
6. The wooden fence rectangle in `town-view-level-8` is empty (the "field at level 9" outline); it looks unfinished.
7. The villager's skin tone equals Trail earth, so heads vanish on the road.

Town as a whole: better than before, still not "beautiful": the villagers and houses now carry it, the field and the flat Thatch colour spoil it.

## 3. MUST FIX (worst first)
1. Grain sack is a Thatch urn and merges with the Thatch hat: make a tied sack, never Thatch on a Thatch hat (villager tunic/hat clash). Affects grain, step-1 loads.
2. Wool bale reads as a white box on the chest at the normal view: round ragged bale on the shoulder with two proud ties.
3. Flour load reads as a drum or barrel: single dark band, tied neck, lying across the arms.
4. Cloth load is Brick on a Brick tunic: force a different tunic or end caps in Limewash.
5. Cottage roofs are blank flat Thatch planes at close (a0 worst): add courses, a sagging ridge cap, a chimney cap, a dormer or patch (section 1).
6. Seams: b and c variants show a dark notch and a floating ridge where wing and main roof meet: add a valley and drop the wing ridge.
7. Wide hat brim (0.45 m) hides shoulders and the back load at close and at step 2: shrink to 0.34 m and move back loads out of the brim.
8. Street picture crops its end cottages: pull the camera back so all five houses show whole (so the "three silhouettes" test can be seen).
