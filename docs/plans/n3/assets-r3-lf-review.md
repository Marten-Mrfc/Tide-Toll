Anything standing where it should not: none on a road tile or in the queue in the pictures I read, but the woodpile at the road edge (level-8) and the dog in open meadow are wrong in spirit. No animal is in the water except the ducks and heron, as meant. The rock is drawn overlapped by a willow.

Does anything draw the eye from the villagers and the roads: yes, the vegetable bed (dark and high contrast on bright green) and the standing bunting poles. The animals are the opposite problem: too small to be seen.

## 2. MUST FIX (worst first)

1. Vegetable bed reads as a pallet or grate: lighter earth, green sprouts, smaller, no dark grid.
2. Rock outcrop touches or overlaps the willow (life-rocks): clear 4 cells round outcrops, no tree in front of one.
3. Ducks read as floating debris: 3x scale, Limewash breast, pair not line.
4. Birds and the flock appear as black ground specks: lighter body, bigger, and no dark dot on the ground unless airborne; picture the lift.
5. Heron not built or pictured at 2.1x: build, picture and read before it is accepted.
6. Dog sits alone in open meadow as a brown smudge: stand it at the market or well, bigger, with a readable head.
7. Cat is a grey blob on the door step: recolour and rescale, or drop it.
8. Gull is invisible at the normal view: scale 2.5x and Limewash, or drop it.

SHOULD FIX

- Woodpiles and benches within 3 cells of a road or queue spot (level-8): move away.
- Bunting poles stand free and dark at the town edge: shorten, recolour.
- Washing line in life-cat stands far from its cottage.
- Meadow light blotches still a touch loud; make flower specks bigger or leave them out.
- Ripe-field earth ring still shows.
