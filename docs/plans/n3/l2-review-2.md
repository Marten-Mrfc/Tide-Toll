## 1. MUST FIX (worst first)

1. **A banner about a level the town has already left.** 1280 u13: "Level 12 / A lane that joins the south cottages." is on screen while the purse says Level 15 of 16 (three levels old). 1280 u33: "A new age" shows at "Level 18 of 28". The progress file says stale level banners are dropped; the picture, taken at 01:29 (after that change of 00:42), shows it is not enough. In `TownHud.Messages.cs` `Large()` only calls `_large.DropWaiting(KindLevel)` when a NEW level banner is pushed: a banner already showing, or taken back by a choice and waiting, is never dropped, and a banner is never checked against the level when it is about to show. Change: in `MessageQueue.Step`, when a Level-kind message is about to start (or is already showing), drop it if its level is below the town's level at that moment (give the message its level, compare with `t.Level`); a "new age" banner the same against the age. Also drop the level banner of a choice level in the same way when the choice opens. Then retake u13 and u33 and read them.
2. **The message clocks run on a capped step, so banners, notes and hints overstay whenever the frame rate is low.** `BookMotion.MaxStep` is 1/20 s and every stay (`_firstAge += Math.Min(dt, MaxStep)`, the queues' stays) is counted in those capped steps. Below 20 frames a second a 3.2 s banner stays up for much longer in real time; at the 1 frame a second of the tour it stays about a minute of game time. This is the likely cause of finding 1 and of 1280 u03: hint 1 ("Your villager fetches water...", spec: at most 12 s, gone after the first coin) is still in the line at t=72 s with 4 coin earned, "Achievement: First coin." in the note section and the Level 2 banner above. The owner's Windows machine can drop under 20 fps in the busy town. Change: count stays and the hint's `_firstAge` in real time (`Time.GetTicksMsec`, cap one step at 0.25 s so a stall does not eat a message whole); keep `MaxStep` only for the easing. And make `TownHud.StripStep` style clock-holding the rule for the tour, so the audit pictures show what a player sees.
3. **Hint 4 is not plain words.** "Scroll out to see the whole page, and the book." The player has no idea what "the page" and "the book" are before they have seen them; this is jargon. Change to: "Scroll the wheel down to zoom out and see the whole town." (If the book must be named: "...and the book it sits in.") Same check for the hint list in `FirstHints`.
4. **The first minute at 1280 x 720 is unseen.** There is no L2 u01 or u02 at 1280. The line is 436 px wide there with 12 px type; hint 2 ("You can afford your first upgrade. Click it in the shop, or press its key.", 72 characters) and hint 3 (73) fill about 405 of the 436 px (hint 3 is pictured in u33 and fits with 15 px each side). Take u01, u02 (and one with hint 2 showing) at 1280 and read them before the owner does. Until then the first-minute layout at 1280 is not proven.
5. **Tooltips, the pointer tip and the strip's second request are not pictured at all.** The builder moved every tooltip into a new control (achievements, boons, goal, strip, note) and says "not pictured". The owner said the popups feel off; a tooltip is a popup. Picture at 1280 and 1920 at least: a shop row (not yet), a shop row with its pill at the rail's edge, the bell, a navigation button, the choice's option, an achievement cell, and Pave mode with its tip. `TipRect` is only tested by numbers.

At W 1641 sheet units (R 320) the room between the rails is 953; the page is 960. Change: page width = min(960, W-64, room between rails - 16).
2. **Hints show while a decision is open.** 1280 u06: the choice of two is open, the goal is blank, an achievement note shows, and the scroll hint "Scroll out to see the whole page, and the book." is in the line: three messages and a choice asking at once. A hint must wait while the choice or a page is open (add to `due` in `FirstMinuteFrame`).
3. **Hint 3 fires 30 minutes late and under a banner.** 1280 u33: "Each level gives the town something new. The purse shows what comes next." sits in the line in the second age (level 18 of 28) while "A new age" shows above. It is also tied to nothing the player did. Once hint 2 is waiting "until bought" nothing after it can show; that is right, but then hint 3 must not outlive the first level.
4. **The zero is a slashed zero.** Atkinson Hyperlegible's zero has a stroke. In the purse at the start (1920 u01) the first line reads "[coin] Ø" and looks like two icons; "Ø of 2Ø", "+Ø a minute", "1ØØ7 Water within 32 s" (u11) read as sign marks, not numbers. It is the first thing the owner sees and a part of "feels off". Use the font's alternate zero (Atkinson ships a plain zero as a stylistic set) or another sans for numbers.

7. **"Reward: 1,333 and renown"** (1280 u11): a coin amount without the coin sign, the sheet's rule 4 ("always carry the coin sign"). Also "Pays 29 a unit" (u33).
8. **The hint ring is heavy.** 1280 u13: a wheat circle about 230 px across, 4 px thick, around a field, on top of 40 walking villagers. The sheet says "thin". Make it 2 px and about 40 percent.
9. **A tag and a coin number touch.** 1280 u11: "+24" ends 24 px left of the tag "A cottage, at level 14". Not overlapping, but two floating words of the same colour in one place. Move a coin number off a tag's rectangle by 8 px, or fade the tag while a number lies on it.

## 4. The first minute (texts from `TownHud.FirstMinute.cs`, `FirstHints`)

1. "Your villager fetches water and carries it home. Every trip earns coin." (until the first coin, 12 s at most). Plain, true, short. It says what happens, not what to do; good for second 0 since there is nothing to do yet. In 1920 u01 and u02 it sits in the line and does nothing else is in the way.
2. "You can afford your first upgrade. Click it in the shop, or press its key." (until bought). Plain and it names both ways. Weak: "its" is unclear when three pills are solid (1920 u02); name the row or pulse it.
3. "Each level gives the town something new. The purse shows what comes next." (6 s). Understandable; says where to look, not what to do. It must be tied to the level banner appearing (should 3).
4. "Scroll out to see the whole page, and the book." (until zoomed out, 8 s at most, after 75 s). Not plain (must 3).

Does it teach what to do? Hints 1 and 2 yes. 3 explains. 4 no. Nothing teaches the bell (it unlocks at level 2; the first banner says "the bell green", the row says "Ring [Space]"), Pave or the choice of two; the choice card does carry Z and X in its caps, which is enough. Never shown twice is in the code (`hints_seen`); I did not test it.
