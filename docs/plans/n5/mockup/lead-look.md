# The lead's look at the first board (27 November, 09:30 UTC)

The owner on `work-now-and-planned.png`: "Right now I find the field a bit ugly etc. Like improve the assets, make it more detailed maybe, do improvements. Make it fit more."

The scenes tell the idea; the things in them are stand-in blocks and it shows. What I see, worst first.

## A field (the worst)
- The ripe rows are flat mustard **planks** with ruler-straight sides, the stubble is one brown slab and the regrowing row is a dark green box. It reads as decking, the fault the style sheet names for the old field ("a pallet of planks").
- A field is **rows of plants on earth**. Each ripe row is a ridge of many short upright tufts with an uneven, toothed top and two tones of Thatch; dark earth shows in the furrows between rows. Stubble is earth with short pale stumps in dotted lines and a few fallen stalks. Regrowth is small green shoots, then taller green, so the three states differ in **height** as well as colour (stubble lowest, ripe tallest). The edge where a reaper stands is ragged, and a cut swath lies behind them before it is bound.
- Sheaves are bound bundles with a waist, leaning together in stooks of three, not goblets.
- The sickles are thin grey sticks. A tool that must read at the normal view is drawn larger than life: a curved blade seen from above, at least 0.25 m thick in its broadest part (style sheet, section 4).
- Keep the present field's footprint and grass rim so it sits in the meadow as today.

## The well
- The wet ground is a hard dark **disc**, like a platform. It should be an uneven, soft-edged darker patch with a puddle or two and footprints toward the lane.
- The worker is half hidden behind the well and the bucket blurs into the pails. Put the crank and the worker on the side the camera sees; show the rope from the roller to the bucket; make the bucket (staves, an iron band) differ from the pails (two, with blue water tops).
- The "now" picture is taken from much closer than "planned".

## The mill
- The flour puff is a white lump with ink lines round its facets. It should be three to five soft pale puffs with no ink line, and a pale dusting on the step.
- The sacks read as pots. A sack has a tied neck and slumps; grain sacks ochre, flour sacks pale; stacked two or three high.
- The door is a black hole. A plank door leaf standing open, a dark inside with a hint of the stone.
- The hoist is small and on the far side. Bring it to the side the camera sees.

## The market
- The best of the four. A stallholder in an apron reads; add one thing that says "selling" (scales or a coin box), and let the delivered goods be told by kind.

## All four
- The lanes are flat brown strips with ruler edges. Use the game's own ground and trail or road look.
- The new things are lit and coloured a little differently from the game's models. Make them the way the game's models are made (the generators under `assets/src/models/`, the palette of the style sheet, real edges for the ink line), so they fit and can be used when the idea is built.
- Nothing is perfectly straight or square (style sheet, section 3).
- Each pair should be seen from about the same distance and angle.
