# Art audit: planned panels of work-now-and-planned.png

Judged from the four single planned panels (800x600) and the n3 reference pictures (field-ripe, field-stubble, life-street). No crops were made (time); pixel areas are in 800x600 panel coordinates. Not judged: the idea itself.

## 1. Field (worst)
Panel field-planned.png.
- Ripe rows, ~(155-640, 235-510): three giant flat mustard planks, ruler-straight, each ~1.5x a villager wide, with a thick slab edge on the near side. Reads as decking. Do NOT reuse the n3 "ripe" top either: it is one tilted plate with hairlines. Build each row as a low ridge of earth (Furrow brown) 0.5 m wide, 0.15 m high, carrying 14-20 upright tufts (stalk cluster 0.12-0.18 m, tall 0.55 m, Thatch with a lighter Straw tip, every 3rd tuft Thatch-dark). Tops toothed, row line gently bowed (wobble 0.1 m over a row), gaps and one missing tuft per row. 6-7 rows across the field, 0.35 m furrow of dark earth between. Close: ear heads as small 3-facet cones (>=8 cm). Normal: only the toothed silhouette and two tones must survive. Far: rows read as stripes; keep the stripe rhythm.
- Cut strip, ~(330-610, 120-300): bare mid-brown slab with two lighter lines. Needs the same ridge/furrow earth, plus short pale stumps (0.08 m, Straw) in dotted lines every 0.25 m, 4-6 fallen stalks lying across, and a swath (a lying band 0.3 m wide of 8-10 angled stalks) behind the reapers.
- Regrowing row, ~(360-725, 75-285): a dark green box along the top edge, flat top, hard rectangle, colour (deep forest green) clashes with the meadow green and with the Thatch palette. Make it a ridge of small Sprout-green tufts 0.15-0.25 m, lighter fresh green at tips, uneven; height clearly between stubble and ripe (stubble 0.08 < sprout 0.25 < ripe 0.55).
- The field disc, ~(140-735, 85-495): big octagon whose rim is a hard-edged lime plate with a visible orange-brown earth plate showing inside the ripe slab; rows do not reach the edge so the geometry looks stacked. Keep n3's footprint and grass rim but make the rim irregular, mix of grass tufts, and let rows run to a ragged edge. The disc is also larger than the n3 field (compare field-ripe-close) so it is not the same object.
- Sheaves, (250-380, 100-240) and (680-725, 300-340): goblet/cup shapes on thin stems with flat tops; too big (~0.5 m, villager-knee to waist), too regular, aligned in a straight diagonal. Make a sheaf a bound bundle: a waisted cylinder 0.35 m high, ~0.25 m wide, tied with a Rope-dark band at 40%, splayed fringe on top and cut ends below; stand 3 leaning together as a stook (apex tied), 3 stooks irregularly placed on the cut strip, not in a line. Vary rotation and 10% size. The tiny loose lumps at (340,300), (445,408), (530,356) are 3-facet nuggets on top of the plank; replace with fallen stalks.
- Sickles, (305-390, 245-290) and (410-495, 240-270): thin grey lines, serrated like a saw; not a sickle. Make a hooked blade: crescent 0.35 m long, 0.07 m broad at its widest (thicker than 8 cm in silhouette from above, rim stroke counted), Steel grey with one lighter facet and ink line, wooden handle 0.25 m in Oak brown. Held low in front of the reaper, blade curved toward the standing crop.
- Reapers: ok, they match the game's villagers; the reaper at (365,255) is half sunk into the plank (no ground contact shadow). Bend them forward at the waist so the pose reads work.
- Composition: add rhythm: rows slightly fan out, one corner with a stook cluster, a scarecrow (post 1.2 m, cross-arm, Thatch hat, ragged cloth) on the lane-far corner, 2-3 crows (like the game's bird animal), a water jug (0.2 m, clay, with a cloth) and a lunch bundle by the lane end, red poppies (4-6 tiny red 3-facet dots, 8-10 cm) on the grass rim, a stone or two at the corner. Lane end gets a wheelbarrow already present at (440-530, 540-590): it is fine and in-style; keep it, add a few stalks.
- Light: planks are lit more yellow and flatter than the n3 field which has a darker face on the near side; use the palette faces.

## 2. Well
Panel well-planned.png.
- Wet ground disc, (355-690, 190-440): near-black brown, perfect circle with ink edge; hard platform. Make a soft irregular dark-earth patch 3 m, two lighter puddles 0.3 m, footprints (small paired dots) toward the lane; edge as the game's trail ink only on one side.
- Worker half hidden behind the well at (565-630, 270-380); bucket merges with pails (440-540, 230-310). Bring the crank and the worker to the camera side; rope visible from roller to bucket (0.06 m is below the floor, so use 0.08 m, dark Rope); bucket 0.3 m with Oak staves and a Steel band; two pails distinct, blue water tops; the three round blue blobs read as pots.
- Roof, (435-600, 135-250): very large, steep, tilted toward camera; plain Thatch plank with a red ridge; roof is bigger than the n3 well-close roof. Reduce, and add 2 beam ends and a lighter edge.
- Lane, (0-800, 115-600): flat brown strips with ruler edges; the T crossing at (300-440, 270-440) is a perfect right angle. Use the game trail look (the dusty pale road in life-street-close), soft edges, wheel scuff.
- Queue at (190-400, 320-590): good, villagers read. Spacing too even; stagger by hand and give one a pail.
- Carrier with yoke and pails (180-250, 160-265): good and in-style.

## 3. Mill
Panel mill-planned.png.
- Flour puff, (370-420, 325-370): white lump with ink on facets, reads as a pillow. Use 3-5 soft pale (Flour cream, not pure white) puffs, no ink line, spread up 0.4 m, plus pale dust on the step.
- Sacks, (225-270, 345-400), (275-315, 320-370), (415-470, 405-505) and (555-580, 310-345): pot-like octagons, with flat lids. Sack = 0.5 m high, tied neck pinched at the top, slumped bulge, 2-3 facets; grain sacks Ochre, flour sacks Flour cream; stack two high by the door. The white ones at (415-470) are brilliant white and out of palette.
- Door, (370-420, 325-385): black hole. Leaf of Oak planks standing open 60 degrees, dark inside with a hint of a stone.
- Hoist, (550-580, 280-350): tiny, far side. Bring it camera-side, beam 0.6 m with a pulley and a hanging sack.
- The sails and cap are the game's own: good. Sails look wider and whiter (cream sheet with ink line) than the n3 mill-close; check the same sail model is used.
- The grass around the mill is bare; add a trodden ring of flour-dusted ground and stones at the foot.

## 4. Market
Panel market-planned.png. Best of the four, agree with the lead.
- Goods, (300-550, 255-440): stand-in octagonal boxes/pots in green, white, brown, orange: apples and fish and cloth are not told apart. Make baskets (round, Oak, open top) of apples (red 8 cm dots), loaves (long oval), cheese wheel, fish on a board; keep palette. Green boxes (380-450, 275-340) are a colour the game never uses for goods.
- Table, (290-570, 250-430): stacked dark crates in front (305-370, 290-415) clash. Tidy to a plank counter with trestle legs.
- Awning, (330-660, 60-305): good stripes and in-style; stripes are straight and very regular; add a slight sag and a fringe, uneven stripe width.
- Stallholder (265-320, 205-310): good. Add a coin box or scales to say "selling".
- Lane (0-690, 245-600): flat brown strip, ruler-straight, cuts diagonally through the panel.

## 5. Does it fit
- Lanes in all four planned panels are flat, uniformly brown, ruler-edged strips: not like the game's soft pale paths. Biggest "pasted-in" tell.
- Colour tone: stand-in props are more saturated and more evenly lit; ink line is thick and uniform on the new props, thinner on the game's models. Pick faces from the palette, use the game's generators, same ink.
- Grounds: no contact shadows or ground blend; add a soft Furrow-brown skirt and tufts at each prop's base.
- Edges: hard geometric (discs, octagons, slabs). Make every edge a slightly wobbly polygon.

## 6. Board composition
- The "now" panels are taken from much closer than the planned ones (e.g. well), so size comparison is unfair; use the same camera, height and angle for each pair.
- The board is scaled down, so the small detail vanishes; the planned panels use the 600 px height differently. Show the normal-distance picture beside each, and crop for the detail view.
- The field "now" is a plain one-state field vs a planned three-state one, which makes the planned seem more detailed; a fair "now" would show the same state.

## 7. Where I agree and disagree with lead-look.md
Agree on nearly all: planks, sheaves, sickles, wet disc, puff, sacks, door, hoist, lanes, same-distance pairs. Disagree: "keep the present field's footprint" is right, but the planned disc is also bigger than the present one. Missed: scale of the sheaves (too big), the lane's right angle, the field's dark green colour not matching the palette, white (out-of-palette) flour sacks, green goods at the market, thick uniform ink, no contact shadows, and no ambient life (crows, scarecrow, poppies, jug).

## 8. Richer details (each at least 8 cm, quiet at normal view)
- Well: moss on the lowest stones, a rope coil, a wooden lid leaning, a wet footprint trail, a tin cup on the rim, a cat or duck nearby.
- Mill: flour handprints are too small; use a flour-dusted door step, a millstone leaning on the wall (0.6 m disc), a cart with sacks, a bird on the roof ridge.
- Market: a hanging lantern, a price slate, crates with straw, a dropped apple, a dog sniffing, a hanging fish or garlic string at the awning edge.
- Field: see section 1.

## 9. Ranking (worst first)
1. Ripe rows: replace the three planks with toothed tufted ridges on dark earth, bowed, varied.
2. Cut strip: earth plus stumps and fallen stalks and a swath.
3. Regrow row: green tufts, palette green, between stubble and ripe height.
4. Lanes in all panels: game trail look, soft irregular edges.
5. Sheaves: bound waisted bundles at 0.35 m, in leaning stooks of 3, irregularly placed.
6. Sickles: hooked Steel blades, bigger.
7. Field rim and disc: irregular, tufted rim, same footprint as the game's field.
8. Well ground: soft patch with puddles and footprints, not a black disc.
9. Well worker, crank, rope, bucket vs pails: on the camera side, readable.
10. Mill sacks: tied necks, ochre/cream, stacked; remove pure white.
11. Flour puff: 3-5 soft pale puffs, no ink.
12. Mill door and hoist: open plank leaf, camera-side hoist.
13. Market goods: baskets of apples, loaves, cheese, fish; no green boxes.
14. Colour, ink line and contact shadows: all new props from the palette and generators, same ink.
15. Board: same camera for every pair; add normal-distance crops; add field ambient touches (scarecrow, crows, jug, poppies).
