# Review of the trades in the game (n7 step 2, parts 2 and 3)

Reviewer: did not build it. Judged from the real frames in `build/wt/n7-trades-game/build/n7/trades/` (all read at full size or cut; the street pictures counted by eye), against `trades-build-sheet.md` (the "Decisions" table), the board, `trades-review.md`, the n3/n4 sheets. "1080", "720", "150" = 1920x1080, 1280x720, 1280x720 at interface 150 percent. Worst first.

## Short answers to the seven questions

1. **Does the screen answer the owner's sentence?** Mostly yes, and much better than the board. The card says "Trades: Carriers 5, Reapers 13, Millers 2, Helpers 1" with a stepper; the villager card says "Hal · Carrier" with four caps; the map tag says "Wenna, reaper"; hovering "Reapers" rings the reapers (`trades-hover-whole-1920x1080`, rings at the well lane and in the fields). A stranger can read: villagers have trades, here is how many, minus/plus for a count, caps for one villager. What is still missing: the one sentence that says it. The hint "Every villager has a trade..." is not in any frame; the bottom line in all 11 whole frames still reads "Your villager fetches water and carries it home. Every trip earns coin." (singular, level 13, 21 villagers), which is the old herd's wording and says the opposite. And no frame shows the town's own default state (everything muted), so I cannot confirm the first impression a new player has (see 4).
2. **The street.** Counted in `trades-street-after-1920x1080` (960x540 view): 9 straw hats (6 on the road, 3 on the field), 1 white miller's cap, 0 kerchiefs, 0 bare heads (the market crowd behind the stall is hidden). In `trades-card-whole-1920x1080` (the whole play view): about 13 straw hats, 5 blue kerchiefs (small, at the crossroads), 1 to 2 white caps, 1 bare head; this is exactly the card's 13 / 5 / 2 / 1. `street-before` (all 21 Helpers): 0 hats, 21 bare brown heads. So 62 to 65 percent of the town wears the same hat. The straw hat is the biggest and the most saturated hat; the 13 reapers on the road walking one behind the other, all with the same sheaf, make the "all the same" picture again, only now in hats. Honest reading: it is a street of straw hats, and to the owner's eye it still says "they all do the same". See finding 1.
3. to 6. In the findings. 7. At the end.

## Findings

### 1. Most of the town is one trade, and nothing in the frames shows why that is right (must fix before the owner sees it)
Where: `street-after` (all sizes), the Trades card in every frame (Reapers 13, Carriers 5, Millers 2, Helpers 1), the tooltip "Members 13 . places 5" (`trades-tip-1920x1080`). Water: "Members 5 . places 2" (`plus-hover-1280x720`). The straw hat is the most visible object in the street, so a town of 13 straw hats and a few blue dots is the same complaint in new clothes. The tooltip makes it worse: 13 reapers for 5 places, "waiting now 0, away now 0": the player is told eight reapers have no place and are not waiting, and cannot tell what they are doing. Fix: (a) the owner's first look at the Trades card must not be a 13/5/2/1 split: put the default (town-filled) state, with the sim's real numbers, in the next frames and show it to the lead; the pictures took a scripted count (Reapers is bold, so it was set by hand) and are not the town's state. (b) Cap what the town itself does: the fill should not put more than about places-plus-a-few in a trade (a trade with 13 members and 5 places is surplus the town should leave Helpers or spread), or the "70 percent of trips at its own trade" target has to be shown with its figure per kind of hat in the notes. (c) Make the minority hats legible: see 5. (d) Say in the tooltip what the surplus does ("8 help at other work when there is no place").

### 2. The first-minute hint is stale and contradicts the card (must fix before the owner sees it)
Where: bottom line in every whole frame ("Your villager fetches water and carries it home. Every trip earns coin."), and at 150 it wraps out of its box ("...carries it ho / coin.", `trades-card-whole-1280x720-150`, `trades-cap-tip-1280x720-150`; known, being fixed). In a level 13 town with a Trades card and 21 villagers this singular line is wrong and is the loudest sentence on the screen after the card. The trades hint that was built ("Every villager has a trade...") shows in no frame. Fix: do not publish frames, or play, where the first-hint 1 stays up at level 13; retake with the trades hint showing at least once (it is the only plain sentence of the owner's idea), and make sure the wrap fix covers 150.

### 3. At 1280 x 720 (interface 100) the Trades card is tiny; the stepper is small for the thing the owner asked to be able to do (should fix soon)
Where: `trades-card-1280x720` and `game-columns` row 2. Heading and lines are about 11 px type, pills about 22 x 16 px, the muted counts ("5", "2") light grey on paper, the caption "Town fills trades" light grey and about 9 px. Readable, nothing cut, nothing overlapping, and consistent with the rest of the card at that size (the shop's rows are equally small), but the minus pill drawn at that size is below the 32-high rule and the muted number is near the limit. I cannot measure the 40 x 28 hit area from a still. Fix: nothing in the card; the build note should say what the hit area measures in pixels at 720/100, and the muted number ink should be one step darker (it is the town's count, and the player must still read it).

### 4. "Bold = yours, muted = the town's" is broken for Helpers, and the frames show no all-muted state (should fix soon)
Where: Helpers count "1" is bold ink in every frame (`trades-card-1920x1080`, `count-strip`: "1" then "0" bold), while Carriers 5 and Millers 2 are muted; `street-before-1280x720` shows "0 0 0 21" with the 21 bold. A Helper count is never set by the player, so bold here contradicts the rule the sheet gives ("a count you set in ink, bold"). Bold vs muted itself reads: at 1080 "13" bold against "5" muted is clear; at 720 "5" is a light grey and readable. Fix: Helpers' count in the same weight as the others when it is the town's, bold never; or give it its own treatment and say so. The reaper's 13 being bold with the tooltip "You set this count" in a frame meant to show the default town is a staging error in the picture; retake with the town's own numbers beside one with a set count.

### 5. The kerchief and the cap do not carry the street; the straw hat carries everything (should fix soon)
Where: `trades-card-whole-1920x1080` (carriers at the crossroads: five 6 px blue dots, no hat edge); `street-after-close-1920x1080` (the miller: a white cap in a red ring, read as a white dot with the apron lost under the sack; the reaper's brim is 3 times the diameter of any other head). Straw hat versus Helper versus miller reads at close-up. At the play view only the straw hat reads as a hat; the kerchief reads as "a blue head", the cap as "a white head", and a carrier against a Helper (dark hair) is a blue against a dark dot: readable but only by colour, as the builder says. Under a load (a sheaf is held in the arms, pails sit below the head) the straw hat still reads. Carrier at the well's crowd: the blue head and the well's blue roof stripe give a patch of blue; I could not find the well's crowd in any frame at the play view, so it is not verified. Fix: the kerchief a size larger and with its Limewash hem, as the round 2 board had it; the miller's cap its Brick band visible from above; or reduce the straw hat's brim by about 15 percent so the three hats are nearer in weight. Check against `hats-round2.png` at true size again.

### 6. The three map tags land on the map together, over buildings and the fields (should fix soon)
Where: `trades-card-whole-1920x1080`: "Wenna, reaper" on a cottage roof, "Amice, reaper" over a field, "Ada, reaper" over the road; at 150 (`trades-card-whole-1280x720-150`) the three tags cover about a fifth of the whole little map and "Ada, reap" is cut by the shop (`game-columns`, 150 villager). At 150 the tag is also under the Reapers tooltip, whose box overlaps and shows through a tag (`trades-tip-1280x720-150`, behind "Amice, reaper"). The tags do not collide with each other but sit on the cottage, and in `hover-whole` a different tag ("A cottage, at level 14", not this feature) crowds the "+14,484" floater. Fix: show the first-three tags one after another (0.7 s apart, 2 s each) rather than together; place them above the head, not at its side; clip to the map area, not the shop; a tooltip must hide a tag it would cover.

### 7. The villager's card: a good card, three small points (can wait, except the first)

Caps are 40 x 32, current is the darker paper with the ink border 2, the liked cap has an ink corner fold. (a) The fold is a 3 px black triangle at 720; at 1080 it is visible, at 720 it is a speck; the line 2 text "Likes milling" says the same, so the card is fine without it, but the caps are the only thing to be read at 720: make the fold 10 units. ## What a still cannot show, to watch in play (answer to question 7)
- The town's own fill: how many hat changes a minute (the sheet allows about three), and whether a villager visibly pops into another hat while the player reads the card. The head swap is 0.3 s; at 30 fps the notes say a step of 0.32.
- The reapers' trips: 13 reapers on 5 places: do eight stand in a queue, or walk to the well? The 70 percent figure has not been given with the hats picture.
- The crossfade at a click on a walking villager: a miss must not close the card (the sheet kept the step-1 rule: a click on the empty map closes it, which is what the owner's "chaotic" would catch).
- The click that lands as a count changes (0.3 s back rule), at 30 fps.
- Fade of the card with the interface; the card first appearing at the first field; the first three tags and the hint in the same second (6 and 2).
- The motion test is failing in every run (banner allocation 29 KB in one frame, CPU gate 6.3 ms on one run): the builder says it is not this feature, unverified on the branch alone; this must be verified before the merge, since "no allocation per frame" is a hard rule.
- The ink check read 0.53 on a first subject in the old run and the cause was not found; the warm-up hides it. Watch a first real load at the market.
- Old saves (every villager a Helper, the town fills within a second): twenty hat changes in a second at once.
