# Review of the trades mock-up (n7 step 2)

Reviewer: did not draw it. Judged from the pictures (`trades-now-and-planned.png` cut and read at full size, the 1920 whole, `hats-now-and-planned.png`) against `trades-design.md`, `notes.md`, the layout sheet (n3), shop sheet v3 (n4) and the style sheet (n3). "Place" = where to look in the picture. Worst first.

## Findings

1. **The minus button will look dead with "Keep filled" on.** Place: the Trades card, every (-) pill. The design says minus frees a villager to the Hands, and says keep-filled gives an open place to an unassigned Hand within a second. A freed Hand goes straight back into the open place, so the count returns and the click did nothing. If the freed villager is marked as set by the player, the design says nothing about it, and "Hands 5" then stops meaning "assignable". Why it matters: this is the exact "bugged" feel the owner named, and it is the one control the owner asked for. Fix: the stepper sets a **target** for that trade. A trade with a player target is never touched by keep-filled; a trade without one is automatic. Show the difference quietly: automatic counts in muted ink, counts the player set in bold ink. Say so in the tooltip ("You set this. The town leaves it alone.").

2. **The Hand wears a trade's hat today, so the hats lie.** Place: hats board, "Now" strip, and the real street in the 1920 whole (many straw hats on the road and at the market). `TownVillagers.HeadOf(i) = i % 3` gives Hands hair, a pale hood or a straw hat. The planned strip only works because the drawer made the Hand bare-headed. Why it matters: a straw hat on a Hand reads as a reaper and a pale hood as a miller, so the player cannot trust any hat. Fix: Hands get **hair only**, in Mud, Packed earth or Soot brick. No hood, no straw hat, nothing Thatch, Limewash or River on a Hand's head. Put it in the build sheet as a rule, and add a test that fails if a Hand's head model is one of the three trade heads or a hood.

3. **The owner's sentence is not answered by the screen.** Place: the Trades card, the Trade row of the villager card. Someone who has never seen it sees "Trades", three names and numbers. It does not say that each villager has one role, that the numbers are villagers, or that a villager can be set one by one. The villager card's four caps carry no words. "Hands 5" sits in a muted foot, with the word "Hands" meaning two things (see 4). Nothing shows who the reapers are. Fix: (a) card title "Trades" stays, but add a second line of caption under it only the first time (the hint), and keep the hint's wording but show it in the card itself, not the bottom line that disappears; (b) hovering a trade line rings its members on the map (the hint ring: wheat, thin, fading); (c) the villager card's title reads "Joss · Reaper" (name bold, trade muted), the same words as the hover tag; (d) the cap row says its current choice in the title line, since "Water carrier" will not fit beside the caps.

4. **"Hands" collides with other words and sits outside the grid.** Place: the foot line "Hands 5 · take whatever pays best"; the Hand cap; the second-age "boatman's hand"; the layout sheet's "+ hand" tag; the shop's Handcart. The most numerous group, and the source for every plus click, is the least visible thing on the card (caption, muted, left, no count column). Fix: rename the unassigned role **Helpers**. Make it a fourth line in the same grid: icon, "Helpers", the count in the count column, caption "work where it pays best" instead of a stepper. Drop the hairline and the foot. The card stays 156 high (40 + 4 x 28 + 4).

5. **The attention budget is broken.** Place: the 1920 whole screen. Solid wheat ("loud") already shows three pills (Villager 1,160, Flour price 250, Regulars 1,197). The "On" pill is a fourth (layout sheet section 7 counts an "On" pill as loud, limit three), and the wheat "current" cap on the villager card is a fifth. It also puts the darkest mark in the card on its least important control. Fix: Keep filled becomes quiet: a small paper switch with an ink check when on, caption "Town fills trades". The current cap: PaperDarker fill with an ink border 2, not wheat (wheat means money and "can buy" everywhere else).

6. **Hat colours collide with tunics, so the hat alone is not safe.** Place: hats board; the drawer picks a green tunic for the reaper and a dark brick one for the miller, which the game does not do (tunics are chosen per villager from Brick, River, Cast-iron green, Thatch, Soot brick). A River tunic under a River kerchief gives a blue column. A Thatch tunic under a straw hat gives a yellow blob. Fix: drop River and Thatch from the tunic list (use Packed earth, Slate roof), and show every hat on every remaining tunic in the next board. A reaper's straw hat also vanishes against Thatch fields; give it a Brick band (shows from above) instead of Mud.

7. **The miller's cap is a plate.** Place: hats board, planned, 3x and true size, rightmost figure. A Limewash disc wider than the shoulders, with a tan dot (the hair showing through the crown) in the middle: it reads as a fried egg or a chef's disc, not a cap. It also breaks the style sheet's "head a third of the height" and the 0.18 m rule of nothing thinner than a detail the eye needs. Carrying flour, the white cap sits on the white sack and the apron is hidden: all white. Fix: shrink the disc to the head plus about 10 percent; delete the through-dot; give it a visible Packed earth peak at the front and a Brick band that shows from above; the apron gets a Brick tie so that it still shows around a sack. Check again at true size and with a load.

8. **Colour and the hat's visible size do the work, and nothing says how many hats mean something.** Place: hats board at true size. Kerchief (blue, small round) vs Hand (dark brown, small round): readable. Reaper: unmistakable. Miller vs Hand: readable only after fix 2. So the set works once the Hand is plain. Why it still goes wrong: the two blue things (kerchief and the pails) are the same colour and the pails are as wide as the head; with a yoke the head is the third blue patch. Fix: kerchief River with a Limewash hem showing at the front, so it is not the pails' blue alone.

9. **The Train row's name changes with the trade, and its name does not fit.** Place: shop, People group (Train the reapers 3, "Reapers work and sell 4% better"). Shop sheet v3 says every name fits at body size on the 320 rail, never ends in an ellipsis, and rows do not change text by themselves; "Train the water carriers 10" is 194 against 150, and which trade is "suggested" is not defined, so the row will rewrite itself when counts change. Line 2 must be one thing like "+874 a minute"; "work and sell 4% better" is two things, and it is not clear if 4% is now or after the next step. Fix: name "Train reapers" (steps number as the caption, dropped whole if it does not fit), shorter forms in the list below. The trade it trains is the one with the lowest level, ties by order, and changes only when the player buys. Line 2 "Work +4% · goods +4%" (numbers of the next step). Add to the fixture: the longest name in the table below.

10. **Swapping the two cards is calm only if the sizes and timing match.** Place: left column, 156 vs 152 high; the villager card replaces the Trades card. The 4 unit difference moves the bottom edge. The design says nothing about a fade. And the Trades card, with its counts, disappears just as the player starts to assign, so they cannot see the counts react. Villagers move, so a click on the map selects and deselects often. Fix: both 156 high; a 0.2 s crossfade in the same rectangle; the villager card keeps one line at the bottom for the trade counts only if it fits, otherwise leave it. Keep-filled never changes the selected villager while their card is open.

11. **The stepper is too small and its states depend on the hit area.** Place: the stepper panel. 25 x 19 px at 1280 x 720, 20 x 15 at 720 / 80. The layout sheet says buttons inside cards are 32 high; these are 24 and the On pill 22. The drawer's 40 x 28 hit area is right; use it, and write it in the sheet so the test measures the hit area, not the drawn pill. Also: the count cell shows only the members, not the places, so the player cannot see that a trade is full and that plus gives a member beyond the places. Fix: the count cell shows "4/4" (members / places), widened to 40; this also lets the status caption go (see 12).

12. **Status captions ("1 helping", "2 waiting") are the weakest part of the line and cause the misfit.** Place: Reapers line. It is muted, appears and disappears as villagers step to other work every few seconds, and "helping" is unclear (helping whom?). Fix: remove it from the line; it goes to the tooltip (fact line "Away: 1"). If kept, say "1 away", smooth over 10 s, fade 0.2 s, in a fixed box.

13. **Icons at 20 break the sheet and the Miller and Hand blur together.** Place: the three lines; the four caps. The layout sheet says icons are 24 on screen (the compact rows of the shop sheet use 20 for another reason). At 720 they are 16 px. The miller icon is white on paper, with an outline only, and has the least contrast; the Hand icon is a brown head: at 25 x 20 px caps they differ by one colour patch. Fix: 24 icons (line 28 allows it), the miller icon in Brick band plus Limewash cap so that it has a colour of its own; Hand icon in the muted ink only. Caps keep their tooltips.

14. **The order of caps differs between the two cards.** Place: Trades card (Water, Reaper, Miller, Hands last) against the villager card ([hand] [kerchief] [straw] [cap]). Fix: the Helper is last in both.

15. **"Likes the sickle" is not understood, and the dot is not a sign.** Place: villager card line 2, and the dot on the straw-hat cap. A sickle is a tool; the player's words are "reaper". A 3 px dot at the cap's corner looks like a notification badge and at 720 is two pixels. Fix: say the work, "Likes reaping" (carrying water, milling, shearing, weaving, rowing), and make the liked cap's mark a small ink corner fold or a double border instead of a dot, with the tooltip "Reaper: likes this work, +5%" as drawn. For the others: "Water carrier: carries water" and "Helper: works where it pays best".

16. **Line 2 of the villager card (drawer's fix: drop "Lives").** Drawing it as "Near the market · likes the millstone" is a change of wording by size, which jumps between cards. Fix: put the like first and drop the home whole when it does not fit (the home goes to the tooltip): "Likes milling · near the market" (about 250 px at 1080, fits the 272 rail), "Likes carrying water · by the well lane" (home dropped on the 272 rail).

17. **The "Keep filled" text does not say what is filled.** Place: card heading right. The design's own word is "Keep trades filled". Fix: caption "Town fills trades", switch quiet (5). Tooltip: "On: free helpers take open places by themselves. Trades you set stay as you set them."

18. **The tooltip as written is a run-on paragraph; the best fix is right.** Place: tooltips panel, first box. Shop sheet v3 explicitly bans a run-on tooltip. Use the second box (title, one sentence, facts: Members 4 of 4 places, Away 1, Bonus work +20% goods +10%, Trained 3 of 10). Freeze the numbers while it is shown.

19. **Zoomed out and the first appearance are not specified.** The layout sheet fades every card but the purse when the interface fades; the Trades card must fade with the ledger's lower sections. When the first field opens, keep-filled changes one villager's hat in front of the player with only a hint at the bottom. Fix: the Trades card fades in over 0.2 s; the first three automatic assignments show the map tag "Joss, reaper" for 2 s (tags are allowed; same style as "+ hand").

20. **The second age will not fit.** Place: design "Step 3": six trades plus Helpers is 7 lines = 244 units against 164 at 150 percent; a scroll hides counts of the very thing the player is assigning. Fix: in step 3 the lines go to 24 high and the heading line to the card's top edge, or the card shows only trades of the age in play (three at a time) and the page turns with the age.

21. **Compact rows in the late first age.** Place: shop at 720. The notes only check this frame (no compact rows needed). The late first age already needs compact rows at 720; one more row (52) flips it earlier, and in compact rows line 2 (the only place that tells the trade's bonus) is gone. Fix: test the Train row in the late first age at 720, and have compact rows keep the name; the bonus goes to the tooltip.

## Answers to the seven questions, shortly

1. Partly: hats and the card communicate that roles exist; nothing tells that each villager has one, or that the card counts people (1, 3, 4). Missing: the explanation in the card itself, the hover rings, the trade in the villager's title, and a fixed meaning for Hand/Helper.
2. See 3, 4, 5, 11, 12, 13, 17. Order of importance on the card is wrong: the switch is loudest, Hands quietest. Grid: right edges of switch and stepper align, icon at 12 and name at 40 are aligned with each other, but the icon size (20) breaks section 4; the count cell is centred, and with two digits it should right-align in a fixed cell. Type sizes are all within the four (caption, body, title). Button heights break "32 inside cards".
3. Current cap (wheat) reads, but wheat means "can buy" (5); liked (dot) does not read (15); caps read as buttons (paper, border, hover states drawn). The swap of cards is a risk (10).
4. See 9. No key: correct. Line 2 is two things: wrong.
5. Misfits:
   1. Status too long: remove the status from the line (12). Card names: Carriers, Reapers, Millers (later Shepherds, Weavers, Boatmen); full names in the tooltip ("Water carriers", "Boatman's hands").
   2. Train names: "Train carriers", "Train reapers", "Train millers", "Train shepherds", "Train weavers", "Train boatmen". Line 2 for all: "Work +10% · goods +8%" (one form, about 21 characters, fits all rails).
   3. Villager line 2: "Likes milling · near the market" (16); drop the home whole when it does not fit.
   4. Stepper: hit area 40 x 28 units, accepted; count cell "4/4".
   5. Heights: accepted, but make both cards 156.
   6. Three trades because the mill: accepted.
6. Hats: see 2, 6, 7, 8. Hand: hair only. Under a load the reaper and carrier still read (the sack and pails sit below the head); the miller does not (7).
7. See 22 to 26.

22. **A count that changes by itself.** Keep-filled, a new workplace or a new villager moves a count with nobody touching it; on an old save about twenty changes over twenty seconds. The count must change with a 0.2 s fade of the digit, never a jump, and a trade changed by the town must not take focus or move the pointer's target (a click that lands as the digit changes: the minus takes the count it showed 0.3 s ago, as the shop's rows do).
23. **Twenty villagers in hats at the well.** A reaper helping at the well wears the straw hat, so a crowd of hats at the well says "reapers" while they carry water. This is the owner's old complaint ("they all do the same") in new clothes. Require in the tuning that at least 70 percent of villager time is at the own trade, report the figure, and let the load (pails, sack) stay the cue of the job.
24. **The card changing while you read it.** The villager card's second and third lines already change; add the cap fills (fade 0.2 s) and the tooltip numbers (freeze while shown).
25. **Ink ceiling.** A group of six villagers is limited to 0.42; the drawer did not run `game-ink`. A 0.70 brim and a 0.50 disc add outline area. Run it before building.
26. **Click targets on moving figures.** Selecting a walking villager is already hard; with the Trades card replaced on each click it becomes a frustration. Keep the card unchanged for a click that misses a villager within 0.3 s (no deselect).

- Heading in the serif with the switch at the right, aligned to the stepper's right edge: aligned (the switch itself changes, 5).
- Stepper glyphs drawn as bars, pill states copied from the shop pills.
- Trade caps: size 40 x 32, hover and current states (current colour changes, 5).
- Train row as a tall row without a key, in the People group after Load; the pill and its wash.
- The kerchief and the wide straw hat as shapes; the apron; triangle counts.
- Drawer's tooltip in the shop tip's form; cap tooltip as one line.
- The notes: honest about what is real and what is drawn.
