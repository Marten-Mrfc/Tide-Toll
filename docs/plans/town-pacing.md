# Town pacing (first age)

Generated by `dotnet run --project tools/TideToll.Tools -c Release -- town --seed 1 --markdown` on 25 November 2026 with the numbers in `sim/Town/TownRules.cs` as handed over. Targets are `TownTargets` in the same file (from `docs/plans/incremental-design.md`); `sim.tests/Town/TownPacingTests.cs` checks them on seed 1. **Seed 1 meets every target with all three bots.**

How the bots play: they think every 0.5 game seconds, buy at most one thing per think, ring the bell whenever it is ready, and stop buying at the charter. Cheapest first buys the cheapest affordable item; best payback first buys the item with the best estimated extra coin per minute per coin, saving for it only when it is under 20 s of income away; random affordable buys a random affordable item (its own seeded stream). "Nothing affordable" counts every moment no shop item could be bought, including when nothing was on offer.

## Notes for the balancer

- Every number is a static field in `TownRules` and can be tried without rebuilding: `town --seed 1 --set "PaceGrowth=1.04;LevelRenown=0,30,80,..."`. `town --detail --seed 1 --bot payback` plays to the charter and prints roads, queues, deliveries and the CPU time of a game minute.
- Level times follow total spending (renown = price), so `LevelRenown` is the main lever for the age's length. The payback bot is about 1.4 times faster than the other two (limit 2).
- Load was the strongest lever between bots (it multiplies every trip); with a base load of 2 units each Load level is +50 %, +33 %, +25 %.
- Pace is the never-ending sink (shrinking steps: factor 1 + 1.5 x (1 - 0.98^level), price x1.035 a level); the bots reach Pace 150 or more by the charter because they buy whatever is cheapest. A human will buy far less of it; fewer, bigger levels may feel better once real players are watched.
- Crowding bites on new trails (comfort 0.22 walkers per cell, at least 2) but hardly on cobbles (0.5 per cell, plus Mason's eye); queues at the well reach 6. If crowding should matter more late, lower `ComfortPerCellCobbles`.
- The late game's slowest minutes hold exactly 2 purchases: little slack there.
